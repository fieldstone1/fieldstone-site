What we offer

Two services. Clearly priced. No overlap.

Sound asset management and thoughtful financial planning are the core of what we do. We keep them separate on purpose — so you always know what you're paying for and why.

Service 01

Discretionary Portfolio Management

Disciplined, discretionary management of public equity, fixed income, and alternative investments. We build and manage your portfolio directly — handling all investment decisions, rebalancing, and tax-conscious execution on your behalf.

Recurring AUM-based fee

Portfolio management decisions may have tax consequences. Fieldstone does not provide tax advice; consult your tax advisor regarding your specific situation.

Service 02

Written Financial Plan

A written financial plan delivered as a standalone engagement. We analyze your full financial picture — cash flow, tax situation, goals, risk, estate, and insurance — and deliver a clear, actionable document you can implement with or without us. Scoped and priced as a flat project fee before any work begins.

Flat project-based fee
Where we add value

Seven areas. One integrated picture.

Financial wellbeing isn't one problem — it's seven that interact. We work across all of them so nothing is optimized in isolation.

What this means

Cash flow, net worth, debt structure, and savings rate — the foundational layer that every other decision sits on. We make sure it's solid before building anything on top of it.

Why it matters

Sophisticated investment strategies can't compensate for a leaking budget or a balance sheet you don't understand. We start here.

What this means

Discretionary management of public equity, fixed income, and alternatives — constructed around your goals, time horizon, values, and risk tolerance using a Total Portfolio Approach.

Why it matters

Most people's portfolios reflect a series of disconnected decisions. We build from a complete picture and manage it as one coherent strategy.

What this means

Tax-conscious portfolio construction, income timing, account location strategy, and coordination with your CPA around events that have planning implications. Fieldstone does not provide tax advice — we provide context and coordinate with your tax advisor.

Why it matters

The gap between a good tax return and a good tax strategy compounds over decades. We help close it.

What this means

Retirement projections, Social Security timing, distribution strategy, withdrawal sequencing, and the question of whether the number you're working toward is the right number.

Why it matters

Accumulation and distribution are different disciplines. We plan for both from the beginning so the transition between them isn't a surprise.

What this means

Estate plan review, beneficiary audit, trust coordination, and working alongside an estate attorney to make sure your legal documents, account titling, and financial plan all point in the same direction.

Why it matters

Beneficiary designations override your will regardless of what it says. Documents drafted before a marriage, a child, or a business sale may not reflect your current life. We help you stay current.

What this means

Coverage adequacy review across life, disability, long-term care, and property — analyzed against your actual balance sheet and financial plan, not against a sales quota.

Why it matters

Most people have some coverage. Whether it's the right kind, in the right amount, structured correctly — that's usually never been verified by someone without a product to sell.

What this means

Understanding how cognitive biases, emotional responses, and life transitions affect financial decision-making — and building processes that account for human nature rather than assuming it away.

Our advantage

Modern financial technology gives us better data and better models. But the application of that data to the unique complexity of a human life — goals that shift, emotions that spike, relationships that change — still requires judgment. That's where we add the most value.

Our process

A repeatable process. Technology-enabled. Built for the long term.

Every client engagement follows the same disciplined sequence — so you always know what's happening, what comes next, and what to expect from us.

How we approach every engagement

Consistent evaluation. Efficient onboarding. Long-term value.

The Fieldstone process isn't ad hoc. Every client goes through the same structured sequence — from initial fit assessment through ongoing partnership — so nothing important gets skipped and nothing falls through the cracks.

Pillar 01

Repeatable client evaluation

Before any engagement begins, we apply a consistent framework to assess whether Fieldstone is the right fit for a client's situation — complexity, goals, timeline, and disposition. This process is the same regardless of relationship size, and it protects both sides from a misaligned engagement.

Pillar 02

Industry-leading onboarding technology

We use institutional-grade platforms to gather, process, and organize client financial information — account data, documents, tax returns, estate plans, and insurance policies — into a complete picture before any recommendation is made. Technology handles the data. We handle the judgment.

Pillar 03

Long-term relationship and community value

Fieldstone is built to be a lasting practice — not a transactional one. Our model is designed around long-term client relationships, ongoing accountability, and giving back to the communities we serve. We measure success by whether clients are better off five years in, not just five months.

Seven steps. Three phases. One consistent advisor throughout.

Phase 1 — Engagement
01

Introductory Call

A 20–30 minute, no-obligation conversation to understand your situation and goals — and to determine whether Fieldstone is the right fit. No pitch, no pressure. We apply the same evaluation criteria to every prospective client regardless of asset level.

02

Proposal & Agreement

You receive a written scope of work and fee proposal tailored to your situation. The fee for portfolio management or financial planning is confirmed in writing before any work begins. Nothing starts until you've reviewed and signed.

Clear fee confirmation before any work begins
Phase 2 — Onboarding
03

Account Setup

Your accounts are opened and assets held directly in your name at an independent, SEC-registered custodian. Fieldstone never takes custody of client assets. We use institutional-grade account infrastructure and digital onboarding to make this process efficient and accurate.

Secure digital onboarding · Assets in your name
04

Discovery & Analysis

A structured deep-dive into your full financial picture — cash flow, tax situation, equity compensation, insurance, and estate documents. We use integrated planning technology to aggregate and analyze this information systematically, so no piece of your financial life is left unexamined.

Systematic data gathering · Complete financial picture
05

Plan & Portfolio Design

For portfolio management clients: a portfolio built using Fieldstone's Total Portfolio Approach, matched to your goals, time horizon, and risk tolerance. For financial planning clients: a full written plan delivered as a clear, actionable document. Both are explained in full before implementation.

Written deliverable confirmed before implementation
Phase 3 — Ongoing Partnership
06

Implementation

Portfolio accounts are funded and transitioned in a tax-aware manner, with trades executing directly through your independent custodian — never through Fieldstone. Financial plans are delivered with a clear implementation roadmap and coordination with any specialists required.

07

Ongoing Reviews & Community

Regular check-ins, proactive rebalancing, and annual plan refreshes keep your strategy current as your life, goals, and markets evolve. Portfolio management clients have direct access to their advisor throughout — not a call center, not a portal. We are also committed to ongoing financial education and community engagement for the families and communities we serve.

Direct advisor access · Long-term relationship

Professional network

Fieldstone maintains working relationships with experienced professionals across the disciplines that intersect with financial planning — so clients have access to qualified guidance without having to source it themselves.

Tax & Accounting

CPA relationships across individual, business, and trust taxation — equity compensation, multi-state filing, and high-net-worth planning. We coordinate directly so nothing falls between the tax return and the financial plan.

Estate & Legal

Attorney relationships spanning estate planning, trust drafting, business succession, and entity structuring. We stay involved through implementation to ensure advisory and legal work stay aligned.

Insurance & Risk

Independent insurance professionals who analyze coverage without product incentives — life, disability, long-term care, and business risk. Brought in when the analysis calls for it.

Business Advisory

M&A advisors, business valuation professionals, and succession planning consultants for clients navigating ownership transitions alongside their personal financial picture.

Lending & Banking

Private banking and lending relationships for situations involving concentrated positions, securities-backed lending, jumbo financing, or liquidity needs that intersect with the investment portfolio.

Other Specialists

From executive benefits consultants to charitable giving advisors — when a client's situation calls for a specialist, we have a process for finding and vetting the right one.

We don't receive referral fees or compensation from any professional in our network. Every introduction is made because we believe it's the right one for the client.
